Security

RedMed keeps your medical profile on your iPhone. NFC tags store a link to your hosted emergency card — not your full record on the band.

  • Edits require Face ID or Touch ID after first setup.
  • Bracelet chip URLs are stored in the Keychain, not plain UserDefaults.
  • 911 and scan flows work offline where possible; GPS stays on-device until you share it.
  • Passersby who tap your band see only what you wrote to the tag.
